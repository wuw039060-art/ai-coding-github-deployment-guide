# v2.3 Full-Book Reduction Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Revise the remaining 98 chapters so all ten volumes total 380,000–420,000 visible characters while retaining 104 chapter identities, advanced knowledge, safety boundaries, and usable operational references.

**Architecture:** Treat each volume as an independently reviewable editorial unit with a frozen baseline and explicit character range. Main chapters keep mental models, decisions, minimal evidence and risk boundaries; eight thematic references own button-level procedures, long commands, configurations, videos and glossary material. Existing structural, pilot and audit tooling are extended with a volume-aware progress gate.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown, Python 3 standard library, `unittest`, Make.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-20-v2.3-full-reduction-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Keep 10 volumes, 104 chapter IDs, existing chapter titles and numbering.
- Final chapter total must be 380,000–420,000 visible characters.
- Volume targets are fixed at: V1 21,172–24,059; V2 34,428–38,254; V3 35,144–38,843; V4 30,683–34,293; V5 26,538–30,077; V6 36,334–41,524; V7 29,062–32,694; V8 37,223–42,806; V9 38,674–42,541; V10 79,600–88,444.
- Keep the six pilot chapters inside their existing `PILOT-v2.3.md` ranges.
- Preserve one core model, one minimal example/evidence set, an evidence boundary, risk controls and a “can skip / continue” decision where the topic needs them.
- Keep no more than one complete story per chapter and zero exact duplicate paragraphs over 80 visible characters.
- Move button-level procedures, long command/configuration listings, full video cards and glossary entries to the eight thematic references.
- Mark volatile prices, quotas, policies, versions and interfaces with a dated verification boundary; do not present remembered values as current facts.
- Do not merge or push without a new explicit user decision.
